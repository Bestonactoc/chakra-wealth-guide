Muladhara · Base of the spine

The Root

This is your ground. Safety, home, and the deep knowing in your body that you are held. When life has shifted under your feet, a loss, a move, a relationship ending, a diagnosis, and you're awake at 3am with your heart racing for no reason you can name, this is where the healing starts. It's hard to trust your soul path when your body still thinks it's in danger.

ElementEarth
ColorDeep Red
BodyLegs, feet, base
SeedLAM
HealsFeeling safe through change
1

What lives here

Read this first

Your root is the part of you that answers one question all day long without you noticing: am I safe? When the answer is yes, you sleep. You make clear choices. You can sit with hard news without your whole body going into alarm.

When the answer is no, everything feels shaky. Maybe you've buried someone you love, moved away from everything familiar, or watched a marriage or a family change shape. Maybe old trauma still lives in your body. Your root keeps bracing for the next hard thing, even on a quiet afternoon. A bill, a phone call, or an ache becomes proof that something is wrong. You get through the day and call it coping.

The good news is your body learns safety the same way it learned fear. Slowly, through repetition, through gentle practice. You are not broken. You are a woman whose body has been holding a lot. Everything below is here to help it finally rest.

When it's blocked

  • ●Wide awake at 3am, heart racing, no reason you can name
  • ●A constant low hum that something bad is coming
  • ●You feel unsettled, even in your own home
  • ●Every change or bit of news feels like a threat

When it's flowing

  • ✦You feel at home in your body and your life
  • ✦A settled knowing that you can handle what comes
  • ✦You let hard news land without panicking
  • ✦You rest without feeling like you have to earn it

Body practice

Grounding breath for the nervous system

Barefoot Grounding Breath

Slow 6-count exhale
1Get your feet on something solid. Bare feet on the floor, or on grass or earth if you can. Feel the weight of your body drop down through your soles.
2Breathe in for 4. Through the nose, low into your belly, not your chest.
3Exhale for 6. Long and slow through the mouth. The longer exhale is what tells your nervous system it's safe to settle.
4Repeat for 6 rounds. With each exhale, imagine roots growing from your feet down into the earth, thick and unhurried. This one works lying in bed too, for the nights you're awake and don't want to be.
Breathe with the circle

Follow the ring. In as it grows, out as it settles.

Svadhisthana · Below the navel

The Sacral

This is your water. Joy, creativity, emotion, and the part of you that comes alive when something delights you. When life has become all duty, caregiving, and getting through, when you can't remember the last time you laughed from your belly or made something just for fun, this is the center calling you back. Joy isn't frivolous. It's how your spirit remembers it's alive.

ElementWater
ColorOrange
BodyHips, low belly, womb
SeedVAM
HealsJoy and feeling again
1

What lives here

Read this first

Your sacral center is where joy and feeling live. It's the part of you that lights up over a new idea, a good song, a walk by the water, or something beautiful you made with your own hands. When it's flowing, your emotions move through you instead of getting stuck. You let yourself cry, laugh, play, and enjoy your life.

When it's blocked, life turns flat and gray. Maybe you've spent years taking care of everyone else. Maybe grief or a painful relationship taught you to shut your feelings down so you could keep going. You feel guilty resting, guilty spending on yourself, guilty enjoying anything while someone else is hurting. The paints, the journal, the dancing, the garden all sit untouched.

Pleasure isn't something you earn after everyone else is taken care of. For this center, it's medicine. When you let yourself feel good again, your creativity, your emotions, and your spark all come back with it.

When it's blocked

  • ●Life feels like a list of chores and appointments
  • ●You can't remember what lights you up
  • ●Guilt around rest, pleasure, or doing things for you
  • ●Tears and feelings get pushed down to keep going

When it's flowing

  • ✦Small joys light you up again
  • ✦You create for the fun of it
  • ✦You enjoy rest and treats without guilt
  • ✦Your feelings move through you and out

Manipura · Above the navel

The Solar Plexus

This is your fire. Confidence, self-trust, and the power of choice. When you second-guess every decision, wait for someone else's permission, or feel like life keeps happening to you, this is the center asking to be lit again. You have more power than you've been told. This is where you step out of the victim role and into the woman who chooses her own path.

What lives here

Read this first

Your solar plexus is your inner fire, the part of you that says I can do this and means it. When it's lit, you make a choice and move forward. You say no without a long explanation. You back yourself, even when family or friends don't understand.

When it's low, you give your power away without noticing. You wait for permission that never comes. You say yes when your whole body means no, then feel resentful for days. You tell yourself you're too old, too late, or not ready for the calling you feel. When hard things happen, it can start to feel like life is something that happens to you instead of something you get to shape.

Confidence isn't something you're born with or without. It's built one small choice at a time. Every time you keep a promise to yourself, set a boundary, or take a step toward your purpose, you feed the fire. You always have the power to choose, and that choice is what turns a victim into a victor.

When it's blocked

  • ●You second-guess decisions for days
  • ●You wait for someone else's permission
  • ●You say yes when you mean no
  • ●You feel like life keeps happening to you

When it's flowing

  • ✦You make a choice and move forward
  • ✦You trust your own judgment
  • ✦You set boundaries without guilt
  • ✦You feel like the author of your own life

Anahata · Center of the chest

The Heart

This is your air. Love, compassion, forgiveness, and the ability to let love in. When you give and give but can't receive, when grief has closed your heart, or when you're far kinder to everyone else than you are to yourself, this is the center asking for care. Your heart was made to love and be loved, both ways.

ElementAir
ColorGreen
BodyChest, heart, lungs
SeedYAM
HealsGrief, love, and receiving
1

What lives here

Read this first

Your heart is where love flows in both directions. It's whether you can take a compliment without brushing it off, let someone help without feeling like a burden, and speak to yourself with the same kindness you give a dear friend. It's also where grief lives, and where forgiveness happens.

When it's guarded, you become the one who gives and never lets anyone give back. You pour from an empty cup and call it love. After loss, betrayal, or a relationship that hurt you, you may have built a wall around your heart just to survive. Grief catches you in the grocery store. An old hurt still sits heavy in your chest. Being needed feels safe. Being cared for feels exposed.

Here's what shifts it. You were never meant to give from empty forever. Letting love in isn't selfish, and opening your heart doesn't mean forgetting who you've lost. The love of those who have passed is still with you. This center is where you soften enough to feel it.

When it's blocked

  • ●You brush off help, praise, and kindness
  • ●You give from an empty cup
  • ●Grief or an old hurt sits heavy in your chest
  • ●You're harder on yourself than you'd ever be on a friend

When it's flowing

  • ✦You take a compliment with a simple thank you
  • ✦You let love and support in without guilt
  • ✦You feel your loved ones close, even across the veil
  • ✦You're as kind to yourself as to everyone else

1Close your eyes and soften the space between your brows. Let your forehead relax. Most of us hold worry and overthinking right there without knowing it.
2Take a few slow breaths into that space. Picture a soft indigo light glowing at the center of your forehead, clearing the fog with each breath.
3Ask the question you've been circling. Silently, then wait. Don't reach for the answer, let it come. The first quiet response, before the doubting starts, is usually the true one.
4Write down what came. Even if it surprises you. You're teaching yourself to notice your knowing and trust it enough to act on it.

Sahasrara · Crown of the head

The Crown

This is your connection. Trust, surrender, and the felt sense that something bigger is holding you. When you're gripping every outcome, when your prayers feel like talking into an empty room, or when you feel alone in the hard times, this is the center that's closed off. You are guided, you are loved, and you were never meant to do this alone.

What lives here

Read this first

Your crown is where you connect to Spirit, your angels, your guides, and the loved ones who have crossed over. It's the trust that life is unfolding for you, even when you can't see how. When it's open, you feel held. You do your part and let the rest come. There's a peace in you that doesn't depend on everything going right.

When it's closed, you carry the whole weight yourself. You try to control every outcome and treat rest like something dangerous. Your spiritual practice slips away when life gets busy or heavy. Prayers and meditations feel empty. After a loss, you may feel cut off from the ones you love on the other side, and wonder what you're even here for.

Surrender isn't giving up. It's doing your part fully, then trusting enough to stop gripping so hard. Life continues beyond this one, and so does love. This center is where you remember you are never truly alone.

When it's blocked

  • ●You try to control every outcome
  • ●Prayer or meditation feels empty
  • ●You feel alone when life gets hard
  • ●You've lost touch with your purpose

When it's flowing

  • ✦You trust that life is unfolding for you
  • ✦You feel your angels and loved ones near
  • ✦You do your part and let the rest come
  • ✦You feel peace, even in uncertain times

Body practice

Open the crown, let go

Palms Up & The Letting-Go Breath

Open posture + a release
1Sit with your palms open and facing up. On your knees, loose and soft. This small posture tells your body you're receiving, not gripping.
2Imagine the top of your head opening. A soft violet-white light pouring in from above with each breath, like you're being filled and held from the top down.
3Name the thing you're gripping. The outcome you keep trying to control or the worry you can't put down. On a long exhale, silently say “I release this to Spirit,” and feel your hands soften.
4Let one thing go today. Loosen your grip on one outcome. Do your part, then leave the rest in bigger hands. Notice you're still held.
